Write a charter

A charter is the contract a role runs under: what it is accountable for, what it may do, the proof it needs, and what it must never touch. Write it as Markdown in the standard shape below — the loop reads it by fixed rules, never by a model. It cannot list questions or steps: those are derived from the charter and your data. Everything here stays in your browser until you run it.

The standard shape, key by key

## Glossary- **term**: meaningwhat a word means in this business
## Accountabilities### id — statementan outcome the role owns
- metric: `column` · - direction: increase | decrease | stabilise · - horizon: 90 days · - priority: 1 · - lead: …the column that measures it, which way is better, over how long; leads are where to look first
## Decision rights### action_type — descriptionsomething the role may do; nothing else is permitted
- autonomy: L0_observe … L4_act · - reversible: yes · - cap: 200 claims per run · - observe: `metric` after 60 days · - approval: chief_actuary · - max value at risk: 10000how much rope, how many at once, how the result is checked, who signs off
## Evidence standard- min sample: 150 · - max p: 0.01 · - min effect: 1.25 · - correction: benjamini_hochberg · - requires causal design / holdout / confound control: yesthe bar a result must clear; ### Override: action_type sets a stricter bar for one action
## Constraints- never use: `a`, `b` · - leaks the outcome: `x` · - only use: … · - sensitive columns: blocked · - notes: …what may never appear in a test or an action
anywhere> a notecommentary for people; ignored

Unknown sections or keys are errors, not ignored — a constraint that is silently dropped is a permission nobody granted. The same rules run in loop/charter_md.py on the server, which reads the charter again before anyone signs it.
