Write a role charter

A charter is a job, not a workflow: what the role is accountable for, what it may do, and what counts as proof. The loop derives the questions. Saved charters appear in the Role list on Try it live.

unsaved

Role

Accountabilities

Each one names a metric column in the data — numeric or 0/1 — and the direction that counts as success. The loop only asks questions that could move one of these.

Decision rights

What the role may do about a finding, and how far it may go without a human. New action types should start at L1_recommend.

Evidence standards

The bar every finding must clear before it becomes evidence. The gate applies this; the model cannot lower it.

Per-action overrides

A stricter (or looser) bar for one action type, e.g. requiring a holdout before anything irreversible.

Constraints

Comma-separated column names. Leakage columns encode the outcome itself; forbidden columns may not be used as features at all.
